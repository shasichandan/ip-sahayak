## Section: Frequently Asked Questions on Patent Applications in India

### Q1: What is a patent in India?
A patent is an exclusive statutory monopoly right granted by the Government of India for an invention to the patentee, in exchange for full disclosure of the invention. The right excludes third parties from making, using, offering for sale, selling, or importing the patented product or process in India without authorization.

### Q2: What are the primary criteria for patentability?
Under the Indian Patents Act 1970, three statutory criteria must be satisfied:
1. **Novelty**: The invention must not have been published or in public domain anywhere in the world prior to the filing date (Section 2(1)(l)).
2. **Inventive Step**: It must not be obvious to a person skilled in the art, representing a technological advancement or economic significance (Section 2(1)(ja)).
3. **Industrial Applicability**: The invention must be capable of being made or used in an industry (Section 2(1)(ac)).

### Q3: What is the step-by-step procedure to file a patent in India?
1. **Prior Art Search**: Search Indian Patent Advanced Search System (InPASS) and global databases.
2. **Drafting Specification**: Prepare Form 2 (provisional or complete specification).
3. **Filing Application**: Submit Form 1 with Form 2, Form 3, and Form 5 via IPO e-filing portal.
4. **Publication**: Automatic publication in the Patent Office Journal after 18 months, or within 1 month via Form 9.
5. **Request for Examination**: Submit Form 18 within 48 months (or Form 18A for startups/women/MSME expedited track).
6. **First Examination Report (FER)**: Patent examiner issues objections; reply must be filed within 6 months (extendable by 3 months via Form 4).
7. **Hearing & Grant**: Attend show-cause hearing if needed. Upon satisfaction, patent certificate is issued under Section 43.
